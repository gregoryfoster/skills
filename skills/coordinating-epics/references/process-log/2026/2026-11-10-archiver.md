### Findings

1. **Brief errors clustered on state nobody had measured** (three of the last five briefs):
   - another service's stamping order;
   - a stream's actual length (the brief priced a 50k-entry replay; it was 7,659);
   - whether a sibling would re-run an export.

   Proposed rule: a decision that depends on a peer's state or a stream's state either measures it or labels it `[unmeasured]` and puts the question to the peer in the brief.
2. **Debt that waits on a world event never discharges by itself.** The "first live send" check waited four days with no qualifying event, and the operator dropped it at close in favour of the shadow-parity proof. Proposed: when such a row is written, also record an alternative proof or an expiry date.
