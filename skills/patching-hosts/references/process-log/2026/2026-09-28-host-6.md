## Session 2026-09-28 — host 6

Host 6 is in a **private** repo, so this entry names no identifier from it. It was the first host with **a primary and an ephemeral worker fleet**. The primary holds local Postgres and Redis, and a restart of its app fails every job in flight. The workers were measured, not patched. Patch only.

### Environment

exe.dev, image dated 2026-04-05. Its security lists were 98 days old. `/exe.dev/setup` was absent. The journal persists, capped at 21 days on a host up for 52.

### Readings

- 222 security updates: 217 in bulk (23.6 min) and 5 held, as their own steps.
- **Packages that restart themselves, whatever needrestart says:**
  - PostgreSQL 16, about 2.8 s, no redo;
  - `containerd`, with containers surviving on their shims;
  - but not `docker.io`, whose daemon stayed old until the reboot.
- The worker image, rebuilt that day, had **7 security updates pending.** Its apt layer came from the build cache, its base tag floats, and nothing rebuilds it on a schedule.
- A warm worker held a broker connection through the reboot.
- Redis started 2.5 s before the tailnet address. The unit's wait covered it, on every boot.
- Docker is socket-activated: its containers stayed down until the socket's first use, 2 m 44 s after boot.
- The root filesystem was at 91%. A 2 GB floor was re-read before each step.

### What the profile lacked

- **A per-package self-restart table.**
- "Enabled" can be false for a daemon in real use, and "active" can mean only that something touched its socket.
- **`who -b` was wrong;** `/proc/stat` btime was right.

### Decisions

- **A held step for a self-restarting package is a service restart,** under the restart approval and the quiesce.
- **"Patched by rebuilding"** holds only when the rebuild upgrades and runs on a schedule. The probe measures an image's pending set in a throwaway container.
- **The quiesce names warm members, not only busy ones.** With no lever to hold work off, it was an empty window re-checked at T-0, at a cost of 0 jobs.
- **The verdict comes from the exit code, `All upgrades installed` and `dpkg --audit`.** The log had about 100 needrestart "kernel versions" lines, about 100 deprecation warnings, `sanity check failed` lines, and one `tar` failure.

### Surprises

- A host LLM runtime installed to `/usr/local/bin`: no apt channel will ever patch it, and something on the host does use it.
- nginx's 0-byte logs since the image date were the dormancy evidence, because the journal couldn't reach back 30 days.
