### What the skill got wrong or lacked

1. **Approval latency closed the window three times, and the gate was right each time.** First, the bulk's run span (2530 s: an 846 s bulk, both held steps, and the chain's 720 s) ended 6 min past a one-off window's end. Then the tailscale approval arrived after the Friday window, already widened to 22:00, had closed. Then the chain's approval came 18 min after the next window closed. Each time, the owner answered later than the fixed end the coordinator had picked. **What worked:** a copy of the knob passed as `--config`, with its `window` line set from the clock when the approval arrived (`Sat 22:00-23:59`). The committed knob was never edited, so there was nothing to revert. This recurs: [host 11's run](2026-10-30-host-11.md) lost a window to a 13 h approval gap. Consider a line in `run.md`: in an owner-attended run, set a one-off window when the approval arrives, not ahead of it. Or have the span refusal print the earliest `window` line that would fit.
2. **A run can pause overnight between held steps, and the docs don't say so.** `gate_recovery` returns early for anything but the bulk, and the dry run's 24 h limit binds only the bulk too. So the tailscale step and the chain ran the next day, 24 h after the recovery point, with no fresh one. The coordinator first told the owner a fresh count and recovery point would be needed; reading `apply.sh` corrected it. One sentence in `run.md` §3 would save the next reader that detour. Whether a data-store restart that late should want a fresher off-node copy is a policy question the scripts don't ask today.
3. **The bulk's span hides its health allowance.** 846 s is 3 × 182 s plus `--health-within`'s default 300 s. A start-by estimate made from 3× alone was 5 min optimistic. The refusal already names the parts; `run.md`'s span sentence could name the 300 s too.

### Decisions

- **A one-off knob copy over a committed window change:** the owner moved the run up three days. The copy keeps the repo's pinned window test, and the repo, untouched.
- **Pause, rather than run outside an approved window:** after the tailscale approval arrived past the window, the run waited for the owner's next word instead of widening on its own.
- **The one-off knob, both post-boot probes and the chain's log** are kept in the run's root-only directory until the recovery point's retention date.
