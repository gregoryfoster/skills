### What the skill got wrong or lacked

1. **The one-origin lane's dry run read the host's lists unless told otherwise, and a bulletin is when they're most likely behind.**
   - run.md's *An expedited run of one origin* said "counted first with `probe.sh --lane origin:<origin> --dry-run-into`", with no `--refresh-into`.
   - The probe already pointed the dry run at the refreshed copy when it had one. So the gap was in the instruction and the gate, not the mechanism.
   - On lists older than the fix, the dry run either counts 0, which the bulk refuses ([2026-10-21](2026-10-21-co-status.md)), or, **new here,** counts an older release that passes every gate: a count of 1 or more, and `origin_left` empty.
   - Only the bulk's `version_moved` (#383) would have shown it, as a line for the owner to read *after* approval 2.
   - A superseding bulletin makes this likely. Here, four more arrived two days after the first, and moved the fix while the lists were stale.
   - **Since fixed** (#493's CR 1296, Wave 5 lane I, merge `e880faa3`): the one-origin lane counts only against the origin's own lists, fetched by the probe in that run, and refuses without `--refresh-into`. run.md names both flags.
2. **The out-of-cycle window again needed a weekday `window` line,** committed for the run and removed in the close (CannObserv/broker `d25fbae`, `b717075`). That's #474's first point, now on a third host after co-index and usa-wa. A weekday line recurs, so only its removal stops it opening again the next Wednesday. **Since fixed** (#421, Wave 6 lane K, merge `c0a11d8d`): a dated `window <YYYY-MM-DD> <HH:MM-HH:MM>` line opens once, and is a `past-window` finding once its day has passed.
3. **The bulk took nothing again** (#474's second point): the origin's one package is a hold group. The owner approved it knowing that.

- **The held step waited for every caller's "clear"**, on one Mayfly broadcast, by the owner's call.
  - The callers joined about 3 hours after the notice, because the channel was relayed late. So the step ran at 21:10Z, in a 19:00–23:00 window.

- **The held step started just after a bus-probe tick,** so the probe's next tick was the first one after the cut.
