### Decisions

- **`Persistent=` affects only `OnCalendar=` timers.** For a monotonic timer, the post-boot check expects the first fire at `OnBootSec`.
- **A pause covers the apply, not the wait for the next approval.** The sweeps were stopped for the apply and started straight after.
- **A dump piped into `sudo tee` reports tee's status.** The gate is `pipefail` plus `pg_restore --list` before the copy.
- **An alert path's downtime budget is per monitor:** the first tick after recovery has to land before `last + interval + grace`. A monitor whose grace is shorter than its interval can't lose a single tick.

### Surprises

No caller retries past a few seconds, so an alert raised while the alert path is down is lost, recorded only on the caller's side.
