### What the skill got wrong or lacked

1. **A reboot chain that aborts at its gate tells no one.** `reboot-chain.sh --approve` returns as soon as the transient timer is set, so the session sees a launch, not the chain's outcome. Here the chain fired 120 s later, into a sweep, and wrote `ABORT` to its log. The operator was waiting for the session to drop. Nothing happened, and nobody noticed for 70 minutes, until they asked to "move the reboot forward". On a host whose `inflight` units run every minute, a fixed delay meets one about 1 time in 20: two 3 s sweeps in every 60 s, plus the drift run. **Since fixed** (#646): the chain re-reads its gate for a bounded time before it aborts, and the launch prints the fire time and the command that reads the outcome.
2. **"Set the window when the approval arrives" leaves the attestation out.** Approval came in two parts: the multiSelect (apply, reboot, pin, healthchecks.io), then 17 h later the owner's off-node sha256s. The window was set at the first part (00:08–03:08Z), and had closed before the second. So the bulk refused, and a second one-off window was set (17:15–20:15Z). This is watcher's lesson again ([2026-11-01](2026-11-01-watcher.md)). Proposed: run.md's rule names the bulk's **last** input, the attestation where there's a dump, as the moment to set the window.
3. **The recovery point was 17 h old at the bulk:** inside the 24 h refusal, but 17 h of check-ins short. That's no harm on this host, which rebuilds its state from the next check-ins, but the gate gave no sign of the age. Proposed: the bulk's verdict states the recovery point's age.
4. **The 3× budget is tight for this host.** Its two bulks ran 2.9× and 3.1× their dry runs. The span still fit, but 3.1× is past the default `bulk-factor`. Noted for the owner, who may set `bulk-factor 3.5`. No change to the skill.

### Decisions

- **A one-off out-of-window run,** chosen by the owner over waiting five days. It used a `--config` copy with a dated window, so the repo knob's windows are unchanged and no `past-window` finding is left behind.
- **Tailscale's pin moved by hand to the newest candidate,** rather than to the release the probe read, with the owner's second approval. The knob change is CannObserv/status#113.
- **The healthchecks.io checks were paused,** not accepted as a `/fail`. Because the first chain aborted, they stayed paused for about 2 h with no gap to cover.
