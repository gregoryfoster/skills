### What the skill got wrong or lacked

1. **The bulk took 4.0× its dry run** (611 s against 154 s). The gate budgets 3×, from replicator's 2.2 and wslcb's 2.5. This set had 85 packages, among them `systemd` (with PID 1's re-exec), Mesa, ghostscript and snapd. The span still fit, since the window was hours long. A tighter window would have let the bulk overrun a quiet range. Consider 4×, or scale by package count.
2. **`--post-boot`'s ordering check ignored `exception ordering:<service>`.** The knob excepts all three units, and the main probe honoured those lines. `--post-boot` still raised `ordering:<svc>` ("its start at this boot succeeded by luck") as findings with no `exception_what`, alongside `ok: null` checks. **Since fixed** (Wave 5 lane I, merge `e880faa3`: `e5f30fbf` red, then `e0ea488a`): post-boot's ordering check takes `exception ordering:<svc>`, and `depends:<datastore>:<unit>` takes its exception for the order alone.
3. **The profile's log-target note.** With the drop-in in place:
   - **after the `systemd` postinst's re-exec,** `get-log-target` read `journal-or-kmsg`;
   - **after the cold boot,** it read `journal`, which `systemctl show -p LogTarget` agreed with. PID 1's lines reached the journal in both cases.

   It first looked like a change in this run's `systemd` update, since an earlier boot under the previous build had read `journal-or-kmsg`. **Read on the host after the run, it isn't one.** exe-init starts PID 1 as `/sbin/init --log-target=syslog`, and systemd reports `syslog` as `journal`. This boot's journal had no reload from PID 1, so the drop-in hadn't been applied yet. The earlier reading was most likely taken after a reload (traced, not re-measured). So the reading depends on whether PID 1 has reloaded since the boot, not on the systemd version, and the `pid1_log_target` reading and the clean-shutdown logic may want to treat both as the journal.
4. **Quiet ranges from timers:** `RandomizedDelaySec=` widened one range by 50 minutes past the observed start times (a daily `OnCalendar=` with a 1 h delay). Worth a line in `knob.md`'s `quiet` row, or a probe hint, telling the reader to derive the range from `OnCalendar=` plus `RandomizedDelaySec=`, not from the last runs.
