### What the skill got wrong or lacked

1. **A one-off `--config` copy doesn't survive to the post-boot probe on its own.** `reboot-chain.sh`'s `next` prints `probe.sh --post-boot ... --config <path>` with the path as given, here under the agent's `/tmp` scratch. The run copied the file into the run's directory before the chain fired. Expected: the bulk, or the chain, copies a `--config` knob into the run's directory (root, 600) and prints that path in `next`. The run's record then also holds the exact knob that gated it. **Filed as #681.**
2. **`recovery-point.sh` asks the owner to name the backup's object, but doesn't show it.** Its `next` says "Confirm the object watcher-backup.service wrote off the node, then name it to the bulk", having just watched that unit succeed. The name had been in the unit's own journal line since its start. Printing the unit's journal lines from its run, or a `backup-object` hint the knob can carry, would save a step that's easy to get wrong. **Filed as #682.**
3. **`--refresh-into` leaves the host's own lists stale, so a by-hand `apt-cache policy` reads old candidates.** Here it showed no NodeSource or Tailscale update, while the probe counted one each. Reading with `-o Dir::State::Lists=<DIR>/lists` matched the probe. **Since noted** in [readings.md](../../readings.md), beside `--refresh-into`.
