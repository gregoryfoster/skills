### What the skill lacked

The bulk's approval arrived at 02:11 the next day, after the window had closed. A second knob PR moved the window to Mon 16:00–23:30. Their expiry also bounded the new window: the bulk had to start before the dry run turned 24 h old. That's worth stating where the skill describes moving a window.

### Decisions

- **The repo's knob test pins the owner's windows exactly.** A temporary window gets in only through a `TEMPORARY_WINDOWS` map keyed to its issue, the pattern of CannObserv/address-validator#347. The closing PR empties the map rather than deleting it, so the next bulletin adds one entry.
- **The owner approved the bulk and the held step together.** A session-only one-shot timer started both at 16:07, inside the window. It stopped itself if the dry run had expired or the bulk wasn't green.
- **A venv's dependency fix went out separately, out of cycle,** in the same issue: certifi, for the same eMudhra compromise. Its deploy restarted the declared `service` at 16:18 on Sunday 2026-10-25, outside any knob window, on the owner's explicit out-of-cycle call. policy.md says such a deploy "takes a window"; this one didn't, and its record is on the host issue rather than in this run.
